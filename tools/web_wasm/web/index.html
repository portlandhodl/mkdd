Mario Kart: Double Dash!! — WebAssembly port (decomp)

Your own disc image is read locally; nothing is uploaded.
Graphics
Resolution, anti-aliasing and filtering apply at the next frame; shader warm-up applies at the next start. 1× with everything off is the console-accurate picture. Auto resolution follows the picture's size on screen (at most 2× with 4×/8× MSAA). Double-click the picture for fullscreen.

Controls: arrows steer · X accelerate (A) · Z brake (B) · Q/W L/R (drift) · Space item (Z) · C/V X/Y · Enter Start · I/J/K/L C-stick. Gamepads work too (standard mapping).
